# Identity

# Instructions

* You will be given a question and two candidate answers.
* If the answers have the same mathematical meaning answer "Yes".
* If the answers have different mathematical meaning answer "No".
* No need to check the correctness of either answer, just whether they are the same.
* Answer with only "Yes" or "No".
* Do not produce any extraneous text and only produce text relevant to the problem.
